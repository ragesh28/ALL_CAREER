The details you need to decide.

Searching one board at a time can miss openings on company sites or in community posts. On LinkedIn and Indeed, the exact years of experience can be buried in the description. All Career puts the posting date, location, and required experience on the card when those details are available.

  1. Find openings across sources

    Browse job portals, employer listings, Telegram posts, and public hiring flyers together.

  2. Compare the details

    Read the posting date and experience side by side. For walk-ins, look for the interview date and location.

  3. Open the original listing

    Follow the job’s application link to review the full requirements and apply. On desktop, review selected jobs before using the optional Auto Apply extension.
