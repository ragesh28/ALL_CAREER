## <a href="#see-when-a-job-was-posted-and-what-it-requires"><img src="./docs/readme/section-accent.svg" width="28" height="19" alt="Link to this section" /></a> See when a job was posted and what it requires

Job openings are spread across portals, employer career pages, and community posts. On LinkedIn and Indeed, the exact years of experience can be inside the description. All Career brings listings into one search and puts the posting date, location, and experience requirement on the job card when the source provides them. [LinkedIn job search guide](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507441/filter-and-sort-job-search-results) · [Indeed job search guide](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/how-does-indeed-work)

## <a href="#from-discovery-to-application"><img src="./docs/readme/section-accent.svg" width="28" height="19" alt="Link to this section" /></a> From discovery to application

1. **Find jobs across sources.** All Career collects openings from Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, other job boards, employer career sites, public Telegram posts, and hiring flyers throughout the day.
2. **Narrow the list.** Search by keyword or company. Filter by source, city, time posted, role, skills, experience, and job category. Walk-ins can also be filtered by interview date.
3. **Open the original listing.** Review the full requirements and apply through the listing. For walk-ins, use the original flyer or contact details when available.

## <a href="#try-your-first-search"><img src="./docs/readme/section-accent.svg" width="28" height="19" alt="Link to this section" /></a> Try your first search

- **New to work?** Choose a city and select **0 years** to find listings that accept zero years of experience, including ranges such as **0–2 years**. Select **Exactly 0 years only** for the narrower search.
- **Looking for a walk-in?** Open **Walk-in Interview** and pick an interview date and a city. If few listings appear, clear the experience filter to see listings where the employer hasn’t specified years.
- **Checking when a job appeared?** Use **Posted Date** to narrow the results. Open the original listing to confirm the role is still accepting applications.

## <a href="#save-or-share-a-search"><img src="./docs/readme/section-accent.svg" width="28" height="19" alt="Link to this section" /></a> Save or share a search

All Jobs updates the URL as you change filters. Copy it to bookmark the search or send someone the same selection. Results reflect the current catalog when they open the link.
