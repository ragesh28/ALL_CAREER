## Scope and limits

- New config contains 35 employers / 36 boards; Booking Holdings COE and Agoda are separate.
- India filtering uses real locations/offices. A search keyword or page URL alone is insufficient.
- Source-provided IDs and links are retained. Board-driven modals are identified separately in the operating guide.
- Short or missing descriptions are recorded, not replaced with general company text.
- The production workflow includes all configured adapters and records failures; registration does not mean every company returned jobs.
- HTTP 429 is respected. Browser fallback does not guarantee access or India vacancies.
- `company-scraper-2-validation-2026-11-24.json` preserves every observation, sample URL and status without full descriptions.
